Everything here is simulated. Prices are a random walk generated in your browser. The wallets, transfers, order book and funding are made up. The live desk above is simulated. The Real-data test uses real Whale Alert transfers and real Hyperliquid prices, traded on paper only. Nothing ever places an order, and the wallet panel can only read a balance. Each bot really acts: gates block signals, Liq Mapper moves take-profit 2, Sizer cuts size, Executor can miss fills. The gates can only help because this simulator assumes risk-on markets drift up, crowded shorts get squeezed and headlines cause jumps. Those are assumptions, not measurements. Post-only entries and take-profits pay a 0.015% maker fee; stops, time exits and liquidations pay 0.045% taker.

Posts that show a bot's best trades with real-looking dollar amounts are marketing. A bot that really front-ran insider selling would not be sold to strangers.
