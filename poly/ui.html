Settings

Each 5 minutes the bot looks at the Up and Down order books. When the cheaper side's ask is inside your price band, it paper-buys that side once and holds it to the end: $1 a share if it wins, $0 if not. Breakeven hit rate = what you pay per share, fees included.

Paper only. It reads Polymarket's public prices and places no orders. It only trades while this page is open (phones pause background tabs). For around-the-clock paper trading, run npm run poly on a computer. Your account is saved in this browser.
